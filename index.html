Four steps, one docket

How your demand gets made

01 · File

You describe it

Open the order form and tell us what you want — reference photos help, but a good description works too.

02 · Confirm

We quote on WhatsApp

An artisan reviews your demand and sends a price and timeline. You confirm and pay via UPI or bank transfer.

03 · Craft

It goes to the bench

Your piece is made by hand, with progress photos sent on WhatsApp so you're never in the dark.

04 · Ship

Packed and sent

Finished pieces are packed with care and shipped anywhere in India, typically within 5–10 days.
